Quick installation

  1. Open Blender 4.5.3 or later.
  2. Go to Edit → Preferences → Add-ons.
  3. Choose Install from Disk and select the AI_Animator ZIP.
  4. Enable AI_Animator.
  5. Open the 3D Viewport sidebar and select the AI Animator tab.

What AI_Animator Beta can do

  • Capture body motion from video and convert it into reusable 3D character animation.
  • Apply captured motion to compatible characters and rigs.
  • Create a native AI_Animator character with a Universal rig and automatic binding.
  • Automatically rig and bind your own humanoid mesh to the Universal rig.
  • Rig and animate suitable humanoid meshes created with AI modeling tools.
  • Create and manage reusable animation files.
  • Transfer animations from Mixamo to Mixamo.
  • Transfer animations from Universal to Universal.
  • Transfer animations from Mixamo to Universal.
  • Transfer animations from Universal to Mixamo.
  • Transfer animations from Mixamo to MakeHuman.
  • Transfer animations from Universal to MakeHuman.
  • Import multiple motion files as an NLA sequence.

Beta limitations

  • Motion capture is available in this Beta release and is still under active development. Results may vary depending on video quality, camera angle, visibility, movement complexity, and character proportions.
  • Motion capture currently focuses on body movement and does not provide full finger or detailed hand capture.
  • Some captured animations may require motion cleanup or adjustment, especially for complex foot contact, rapid movement, occlusion, or unusual poses.
  • MakeHuman is currently supported as a target, not as a tested source.
  • Custom meshes must be clean humanoids in a T-pose or wide A-pose.
  • Automatic rigging and skinning may require manual adjustments on unusual meshes or extreme proportions.
  • Unknown or heavily modified rig structures are not guaranteed to work.

AI_Animator is currently offered as a Beta. Motion capture, animation cleanup, rig compatibility, and workflow tools will continue to improve during development.
